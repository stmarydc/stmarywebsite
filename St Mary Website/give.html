Questions

About giving

Is my gift tax-deductible?

St. Mary is a non-profit church, and gifts are generally tax-deductible as allowed by law.

How do I get a giving statement?

Members can view and download their giving history any time by signing in to CCB. Year-end statements are also emailed each January.

Can I set up recurring giving?

Yes. Choose “recurring” in the Pushpay form to give weekly, every two weeks, or monthly. You can change or stop it any time.

Is online giving secure?

Online gifts are processed by Pushpay, a payment provider used by many churches. St. Mary never sees or stores your full card or bank details.
