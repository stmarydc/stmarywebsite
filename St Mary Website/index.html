Welcome Home

An ancient faith, a family for today

Founded in 2020, St. Mary serves the Washington D.C. metropolitan area from our home in Bethesda, Maryland. We worship in the unbroken tradition of the Church of Alexandria, founded by St. Mark the Apostle.

Whether you grew up Coptic, are exploring Orthodoxy for the first time, or are simply looking for a place to pray, you are welcome here. We openly welcome people of every race and nationality.

  • Salvation through Christ
  • Loving our neighbor
  • A life of service
  • Welcoming every nation

First Visit?

Come and see

You don’t need to know anything to join us. Here’s what to expect on a Sunday morning.

  1. Arrive any time

    The Liturgy is long (around three hours), and many families arrive as it goes on. Slip in quietly and find a seat.

  2. Stand, sit, and simply pray

    We stand for the Gospel and key prayers and sit for readings and the sermon. Follow along with those around you.

  3. Stay for fellowship

    Holy Communion is received by Orthodox Christians who have prepared. Everyone is welcome to receive a blessing and join us afterward.
